How it runs

Four steps, no surprises

The order matters, so it's numbered. Each step ends with something you can see and sign off on before the next one starts.

01

Brief

A call, then a written scope. What it does, who it's for, what "done" means, and what it will cost. No estimate ranges that quietly double.

02

Blueprint

Wireframes, data model and a clickable prototype. You approve the shape of the thing before anyone writes production code.

03

Build

Weekly builds on a staging URL. You watch it come together and redirect early, instead of reviewing a finished thing you'd change.

04

Launch

Deploy, monitoring, handover docs. Thirty days of fixes included, then a support arrangement only if you want one.
